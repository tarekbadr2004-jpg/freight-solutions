International Moving

An international relocation can be one of life’s most stressful experiences. Throughout the entire process, you will need to make countless decisions. We have carefully designed our relocation journey to reduce these stresses. Allowing our customers to focus on what’s important, while we deal with all the finer details.

We at Freight Solutions Global relocation solutions covering export packing, international transportation, customs coordination, destination services, and final delivery.

Packing Services

Freight Solutions professional packing and unpacking services can accommodate any need you’re looking for. Our trained teams will carefully pack your household goods, disassemble furniture, and protect fragile materials for transport. We also provide unpacking, reassembly and put-away services at destination.

Unique / Specialty Items

Your belongings are more than just stuff—they represent a lifetime of precious items, heirlooms, and memories. Freight Solutions are trained and skilled in packing items appropriately and make sure that every item in your moving van is secured and stored safely in transit, including odd-shaped, heavy, or unique items, such as:

  • Pianos
  • Exercise equipment (treadmills, rowers)
  • Unique and heirloom furniture
  • Art pieces
