Project overview

Project overview

During COVID-19, Freight Solutions dismantled, export-packed and coordinated the return of a complete manufacturing facility from Egypt to Turkey, delivering every asset safely.

The challenge

The challenge

During the COVID-19 pandemic, a Turkish manufacturing company decided to cease its operations in Egypt and relocate its production assets back to Turkey. The project involved dismantling, professionally packing, and preparing heavy industrial machinery and equipment for international transportation. Every machine required specialized export packing to ensure it could withstand handling, loading, sea transportation, and arrival at its final destination without damage.

Our solution

Our solution

Freight Solutions managed the project from planning through execution. Our team coordinated the professional packing of all machinery using export-grade packing materials and techniques, ensuring every asset was properly protected before shipment. We supervised the handling, loading, transportation, and export logistics while maintaining close coordination throughout every stage of the project.

The outcome

The outcome

The entire factory relocation was completed safely and efficiently, with all machinery prepared for international transport according to professional export standards. The project demonstrated Freight Solutions' ability to manage complex industrial relocation projects, even under the exceptional challenges presented during the COVID-19 pandemic.
