Who we are

Who we are

Freight Solutions is a trusted provider of integrated logistics, freight forwarding, and relocation solutions, serving businesses and individuals across local and international markets.

Building on our strong foundation in freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, storage, and cargo insurance, we have expanded our expertise to include professional residential and corporate moving services. From office relocations and household moves to international shipping and specialized logistics projects, we deliver end-to-end solutions tailored to each client’s unique requirements.

Driven by professionalism, operational excellence, and customer satisfaction, our experienced team is committed to delivering safe, efficient, and reliable services while ensuring every project is carefully planned, professionally managed, and successfully executed.

Services

Our services

View all services

Air Freight

When speed, reliability, and flexibility are essential, air freight is the ideal solution for time-sensitive shipments. Freight Solutions provides comprehensive air freight services for import and export cargo, supported by a global network of airline partners. Our experienced team manages every stage of the shipment, from planning and documentation to customs clearance and final delivery, ensuring your cargo reaches its destination safely and on schedule.

Sea Freight

Sea freight remains one of the most efficient and cost-effective solutions for international trade. Whether shipping Full Container Loads (FCL), Less than Container Loads (LCL), or specialized cargo, Freight Solutions manages every stage of the shipment with careful planning, reliable carrier coordination, and complete visibility from origin to destination. We support both import and export shipments, providing end-to-end coordination, customs clearance, documentation, and inland transportation to ensure cargo moves safely, efficiently, and on schedule.

Land Freight

Reliable land transportation plays a vital role in connecting ports, airports, warehouses, and customer facilities. Freight Solutions provides dependable land freight services across all Egyptian governorates, supporting import, export, and domestic transportation requirements. We coordinate transportation efficiently to ensure cargo moves safely, on time, and according to each customer’s operational needs.

Customs Clearance

Efficient customs clearance is essential to keeping supply chains moving. Freight Solutions provides professional customs clearance services for import and export shipments across Egyptian seaports, airports, dry ports, and free zones. Our experienced team manages customs documentation, regulatory requirements, and operational coordination to help customers reduce delays and ensure compliance with applicable regulations.

Warehousing

Reliable warehousing is more than storing cargo, it is about keeping your supply chain efficient, organized, and ready to move. Freight Solutions provides secure and flexible warehousing solutions designed to support importers, exporters, manufacturers, distributors, and businesses with short-term and long-term storage requirements. Our warehousing services integrate seamlessly with freight forwarding, customs clearance, and inland transportation, giving customers a complete end-to-end logistics solution.

Specialized Logistics

Some logistics challenges demand far more than standard freight forwarding. They require technical expertise, careful planning, regulatory compliance, and flawless execution. From oversized industrial equipment to dangerous goods and time-critical aviation shipments, Freight Solutions delivers specialized logistics solutions designed to move complex cargo safely, efficiently, and reliably.

Moving & Relocation

We deliver professionally managed relocation services tailored to corporate organizations, government entities, financial institutions, and residential customers, ensuring every move is planned, executed, and completed with precision.

Insurance

Protecting valuable cargo is an important part of every successful logistics operation. Through our trusted insurance partners, Freight Solutions helps businesses and individuals arrange appropriate insurance coverage for shipments, storage, and relocation projects.
