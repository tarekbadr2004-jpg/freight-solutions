06What is the difference between FCL and LCL shipping?

FCL (Full Container Load) means one customer exclusively uses an entire shipping container. LCL (Less than Container Load) allows multiple customers to share one container, making it an economical solution for smaller shipments.

07Do you provide warehousing solutions?

Yes. We offer warehousing services to support inventory management, temporary storage, and distribution requirements.

Frequently asked questions

For individuals and families

19How much does a local move cost?

The cost of a local move depends on several factors, including:

  • The size of your home
  • The number of rooms
  • The volume of furniture and personal belongings
  • Packing requirements
  • Accessibility (stairs, elevators, parking)
  • The number of movers required
  • The number and size of vehicles needed
  • Any specialty items requiring extra care

To provide the most accurate and transparent quotation, we recommend scheduling a free in-home or virtual moving survey with one of our moving specialists.

20What is a free virtual moving survey?

A virtual moving survey is a convenient alternative to an in-home visit. Using your smartphone or tablet, you'll connect with one of our moving consultants through a secure video call. During the survey, you'll simply walk through your home while showing the items you plan to move.

This allows us to accurately estimate:

  • Moving volume
  • Packing requirements
  • Required manpower
  • Truck size
  • Special handling requirements

It's fast, convenient, and saves you time without compromising accuracy.

21What is a free in-home moving survey?

One of our moving specialists visits your home to evaluate your move in person. During the visit, we will:

  • Review the items to be moved
  • Assess access points and parking
  • Identify any fragile or specialty items
  • Discuss your moving schedule
  • Recommend the best moving plan
  • Provide a detailed quotation

This helps ensure efficient planning and a smooth moving experience.

22What moving services do you provide?

Freight Solutions offers complete residential and corporate moving services, including:

  • Professional packing and unpacking
  • Furniture protection
  • Disassembly and reassembly of standard furniture
  • Loading and unloading
  • Transportation using closed moving trucks
  • Office relocation
  • IT equipment relocation
  • International moving
  • Storage solutions
  • Debris removal after unpacking

We tailor every move to your specific requirements.

23What additional services do you offer?

For specialized moving requirements, we can arrange services such as:

  • Fine art crating
  • Antique handling
  • Piano moving
  • Safe moving
  • Wall-mounted TV removal and installation
  • Appliance disconnection and reconnection
  • Custom wooden crates

Any additional services will be discussed during your survey and included in your quotation.

24What packing materials do you use?

Protecting your belongings is one of our highest priorities. We use professional moving materials, including:

  • Corrugated cardboard rolls
  • Bubble wrap
  • Foam rolls
  • Stretch film
  • Heavy-duty packing tape
  • Packing paper
  • Moving blankets
  • Carton boxes in various sizes
  • Wooden crates
  • Hanging wardrobe boxes
  • Labels and identification stickers

Specialized packing materials are also available for fragile, valuable, or oversized items.

25How should I prepare for moving day?

To help your move go as smoothly as possible, we recommend:

  • Decluttering unwanted items
  • Labeling boxes by room
  • Separating important documents and valuables
  • Preparing a small essentials bag
  • Ensuring parking access for the moving truck
  • Informing building management if necessary

Our team can also provide a moving checklist before your scheduled move.

26What items cannot be transported?

For safety and legal reasons, moving companies cannot transport certain items, including:

  • Flammable materials
  • Explosives
  • Gas cylinders
  • Fireworks
  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Perishable food
  • Certain plants (especially for long-distance moves)
  • Cash
  • Jewelry
  • Passports
  • Important personal documents

If you're unsure about a specific item, simply ask our team before moving day.

27Do you move heavy or specialty items?

Yes. We regularly move:

  • Safes
  • Pianos
  • Antique furniture
  • Fine art
  • Marble pieces
  • Large appliances
  • Commercial equipment
  • Delicate items

Some specialty items may require custom crating or additional equipment, which will be identified during your survey.

28Are my belongings protected during the move?

Absolutely. Our trained moving teams use professional packing techniques, protective wrapping, and secure transportation methods to minimize the risk of damage. If additional insurance options are available for high-value items, our team will explain them during the quotation process.

29Do you offer storage solutions?

Yes. We provide secure short-term and long-term storage solutions for customers who need temporary storage before, during, or after their move.

30Do you move internationally?

Yes. Freight Solutions provides international household relocation services through our trusted global logistics network. We can assist with:

  • Export packing
  • International transportation
  • Customs documentation
  • Customs clearance
  • Destination coordination
  • Door-to-door relocation solutions
31How long does a move usually take?

Every move is different. The duration depends on:

  • Property size
  • Distance
  • Access conditions
  • Packing requirements
  • Number of items
  • Weather conditions

Your moving consultant will provide an estimated schedule during your survey.

32Do I need to be present during the move?

We recommend that you or an authorized representative be present during both loading and unloading to answer any questions and verify the inventory.

33Can you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Our team can dismantle and reassemble most standard furniture, including beds, wardrobes, dining tables, desks, and similar items as part of the moving service.
